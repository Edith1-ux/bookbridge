About BookBridge

An official academic initiative by R. C. Technical Institute, Ahmedabad.

BookBridge was founded to eliminate the financial burden of purchasing expensive GTU diploma textbooks every semester. By enabling students across all departments to buy, sell, exchange, and donate books locally, we build a supportive campus ecosystem that reduces paper waste and empowers peer learning.

Free Donations
Senior students donate textbooks to juniors, creating a cycle of giving that benefits every generation.
AI-Powered
Smart recommendations, automated tagging, and intelligent search powered by artificial intelligence.
Secure & Trusted
Verified student accounts, secure messaging, and rating system ensure safe transactions.
