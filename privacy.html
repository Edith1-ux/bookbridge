Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Effective for all BookBridge users

1. Information We Collect

When you register on BookBridge, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your enrollment number and college branch/semester
  • Optional: WhatsApp number for connecting with other students
  • Books you list, exchange requests, and wishlist items

We do not collect payment information — all transactions between students are peer-to-peer.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account securely using JWT tokens
  • To display your listings to other students
  • To allow other students to contact you about book listings
  • To show you relevant book recommendations
  • To maintain platform statistics (anonymised)

3. Data Storage & Security

Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms (SHA-256 / bcrypt) before storage.

Access tokens (JWT) expire within 7 days and are stored only in your browser's localStorage. We recommend logging out from shared devices.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Your contact information (name + WhatsApp) is only shared when you choose to connect with another student through a book listing.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

BookBridge uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store your session token and book preferences. No tracking cookies are used.

6. Your Rights

  • Access — You can view all your data from your dashboard
  • Delete — You can delete your account by contacting us
  • Correction — You can update your profile at any time

7. Contact

For privacy-related queries, email us at support@bookbridge.in or use our contact form.
